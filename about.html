Our Story

LPG Equipment Manufacturer
Since 1995 — Gujranwala

AAA Gas Engineering is a proud branch of Major Enterprises, an established LPG equipment manufacturer in Gujranwala, Punjab. Major Enterprises has made LPG valves since 1995. In 2020 we launched AAA Gas Engineering to build LPG vaporizers, and we have since manufactured 5,000+ units.

Our factory is based in Gujranwala — we manufacture LPG vaporizers (10–900 kg/h), cylinders, valves, and regulators and deliver across all of Pakistan including Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Faisalabad, Multan and Peshawar.

Every LPG vaporizer is OGRA approved, ISO 9001 certified, and PEC registered — the highest standards in Pakistan.

Our Journey

28+ Years of LPG Equipment Manufacturing

1995
Foundation — Major Enterprises
Major Enterprises established in Gujranwala. Started manufacturing LPG cylinder valves for the local market.
2000
Product Expansion
Expanded to LPG regulators and pressure control equipment. Customer base grew across Punjab.
2005
ISO 9001 Certification
Achieved ISO 9001 quality management certification. Upgraded manufacturing facility with precision CNC equipment.
2010
OGRA Approval
Received official OGRA approval for all LPG equipment. Began supplying large industrial clients — textile factories, hospitals, hotels.
2020
LPG Vaporizer Manufacturing Launched
Established AAA Gas Engineering as a dedicated LPG vaporizer brand under Major Enterprises, building water bath and dry type LPG vaporizers.
2025+
Pakistan-Wide + International
5,000+ LPG vaporizers manufactured for customers across Pakistan. Expanding into Middle East and international markets.
