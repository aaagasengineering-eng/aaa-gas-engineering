How It Works

How Does an LPG Vaporizer Work?

An LPG vaporizer converts liquid-phase LPG from storage tanks into vapor phase for industrial burners, boilers, and processes. When natural vaporization is insufficient — especially in cold weather or with high consumption — an LPG vaporizer provides continuous, reliable gas supply.

AAA Gas Engineering's electric water-bath LPG vaporizer uses an immersion heater to warm a water bath, which transfers heat to a stainless steel coil carrying liquid LPG. The LPG vaporizes inside the coil and exits as gas at controlled pressure — safe, automatic, continuous.
