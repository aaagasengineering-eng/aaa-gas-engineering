Frequently Asked Questions
About LPG Vaporizers

What is an LPG vaporizer?
An LPG vaporizer is a device that converts liquid-phase LPG into gas (vapor) for industrial and commercial use. When natural vaporization is insufficient — such as in factories, mills, and hospitals — an LPG vaporizer ensures an uninterrupted gas supply. AAA Gas Engineering manufactures LPG vaporizers from 10 to 900 kg/h capacity.

Full guide: How LPG Vaporizer Works →
What is the difference between Water Bath and Dry Type LPG vaporizer?
A Water Bath LPG Vaporizer uses electrically heated water to vaporize liquid LPG — the most common type in Pakistan. A Dry Type LPG Vaporizer uses direct electric heating elements with no water required — more compact, lower maintenance, and faster startup. AAA Gas Engineering is the only manufacturer in Pakistan offering Dry Type LPG vaporizers.

Is OGRA approval required for LPG vaporizers in Pakistan?
